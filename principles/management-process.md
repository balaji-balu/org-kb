# Principles — Management, process, models and methods

Derived from SWEBOK v4.0 knowledge area(s): Software Engineering Management, Software Engineering Process, Software Engineering Models and Methods. Wording paraphrases SWEBOK; see the [principles index](/principles/index.md) for the full set.

## MGT-1 Plan with estimates, risks and uncertainty made visible

Estimate from historical data, state ranges not single points, and keep a living risk register.

- **Enforcement:** guidance only; reviewed by humans.

## MGT-2 Track progress against objective measures

Monitor scope, schedule, cost and quality with agreed metrics; re-plan when reality diverges.

- **Enforcement:** guidance only; reviewed by humans.

## PRC-1 Fit the process to the context

Choose and tailor a lifecycle (iterative, agile, plan-driven, hybrid) to the project's risk, size and regulatory needs.

- **Enforcement:** guidance only; reviewed by humans.

## PRC-2 Improve the process continuously

Run retrospectives, measure outcomes, and change one thing at a time based on evidence.

- **Enforcement:** guidance only; reviewed by humans.

## MOD-1 Model to understand, communicate and verify

Use models (UML, C4, state, data) at the level of rigor the problem needs; keep them consistent with the code or retire them.

- **Enforcement:** guidance only; reviewed by humans.
