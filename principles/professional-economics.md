# Principles — Professional practice and economics

Derived from SWEBOK v4.0 knowledge area(s): Software Engineering Professional Practice, Software Engineering Economics. Wording paraphrases SWEBOK; see the [principles index](/principles/index.md) for the full set.

## PRO-1 Act in the public interest

Put safety, privacy and the well-being of users and society ahead of convenience, following the IEEE-CS/ACM Software Engineering Code of Ethics.

- **Enforcement:** guidance only; reviewed by humans.

## PRO-2 Communicate honestly and collaborate

Report status, risks and limitations truthfully; review others' work constructively and accept review of your own.

- **Enforcement:** guidance only; reviewed by humans.

## PRO-3 Own your decisions and keep learning

Document reasoning so others can challenge it, stay within your competence, and keep skills current.

- **Enforcement:** guidance only; reviewed by humans.

## ECO-1 Decide on value, not just cost

Compare alternatives by lifecycle cost, benefit, risk and time-to-value (including build vs buy and cost of delay), and revisit the decision when assumptions change.

- **Enforcement:** guidance only; reviewed by humans.
